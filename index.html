Project 01 · Quality Intelligence · 01 The problem

Finding the right report is part of the investigation.

When production downtime occurs, operators can spend too long identifying the defect and deciding which report to pull. Newer engineers reportedly take longer than experienced engineers, and relevant information is split across two systems.

What we know so far

Earlier project discussion reported a 50-minute median for newer engineers and 22 minutes for experienced engineers. Wrong record or parameter selection and weak SOP-to-information links were also reported.

These figures and observations still need source, cohort, sample and time-window verification. Downtime impact has not been quantified.

The user’s job to be done

When downtime occurs and I have a clue about the defect, help me identify it and verify the right report, record and parameter so I can continue the investigation faster without guessing or lowering accuracy.
Evidence boundary: the summary comes from earlier project discussion, not a verbatim customer interview in this thread. The clickable demo is a concept, not a live manufacturing system.
02 · Product decisions

Investigate first. Choose a small, testable solution.

I used the Double Diamond as a way to explain the work: understand the challenge, define the problem, develop and test options, then plan safe delivery.

Discover

Understand the task

Map who investigates, where time is lost, which systems and SOPs are used, and when people need help.

Define

Make success clear

Frame the need as finding and confirming the correct record and parameter without reducing accuracy.

Develop

Compare approaches

Start with guided retrieval and human confirmation; validate the flow before investing in prediction.

Deliver

Gate release on evidence

Define metrics, source dependencies, quality tests, pilot access and incident response before release.

01

Search before guidance

The engineer needs to identify the relevant record before an SOP or investigation guidance can be trusted.

02

One clue, filters optional

Start with what the engineer knows—ID, part, line, symptom or parameter—then narrow results if needed.

03

Defer broad AI prediction

The current evidence points to retrieval and selection problems. Prediction need, data readiness and validation are not established.

03 · Proposed experience

Keep the engineer in control of every decision.

The prototype flow makes the source and uncertainty visible, and it withholds guidance until the user confirms both the record and the parameter.

Search

Enter one clue. Search approved sources with optional filters.

Compare records

See source, key details and why each candidate matched.

Review & confirm

Select the record and parameter. Change either before confirming.

Open guidance

Show approved guidance only after confirmation. Offer safe next steps for ambiguity or outages.

Exception paths are part of the design

Exact match, contextual match, ambiguous results, no results and a source outage each need a clear explanation and next action. A failed source must not look like a complete search.

Prototype feedback changed the requirements

In the demo, the fictional ID NC-2048 returned four candidates without explaining why. The next revision should show match reasons, distinguish exact from contextual evidence and keep sample IDs consistent.

04 · Success and guardrails

Measure speed without rewarding unsafe shortcuts.

Definitions must be agreed before collecting evidence. Prototype-task results and operational downtime outcomes are different measures.

Primary

Time to correct information

For a prototype task, time from receiving a standard defect clue to confirming the correct approved record and parameter. Define operational start/stop events separately.

Success signal

Supervisor escalations

Track escalations per eligible investigation start. Have a quality SME distinguish avoidable from necessary escalation; never discourage quality or safety escalation.

Diagnostic

Completion and drop-off

Correct record-and-parameter confirmations divided by all eligible starts. Report wrong selections, incomplete searches, abandonment and source failures separately.

Accuracy guardrail

Verify the stated 96% before using it as a release threshold.

Use an adjudicated answer set to define correct record-and-parameter selection. Confirm the 96% source, denominator, sample and method with a quality SME; stop pilot progression if the agreed guardrail is breached.

06 · Governance and rollout

A proposed correction must never silently become a production change.

The correction workflow remains separate from core search and enters a pilot only if need, feasibility and capacity are demonstrated.

Separate rolesEngineer proposes; an independent reviewer decides; a separate authorized publisher applies an approved change.
Trace every versionRecord old/new values, evidence, rationale, actors, timestamps, decision and a controlled rollback.
Pause on any breachZero unapproved production mapping changes. Any occurrence pauses rollout and publishing and triggers investigation.

Resume only after the cause, impact, corrective action and designated quality/system-owner approval are documented. Pilot access requires tests, role separation, audit traceability, rollback rehearsal, a named owner and entry/exit criteria.
