# LaunchLane

LaunchLane is a portfolio concept for coordinating B2B SaaS customer onboarding with shared milestones, clear owners, and blocker visibility.

- [Open the interactive prototype](https://manishsunkapur.github.io/launchlane/)
- [Read the product case study](https://manishsunkapur.github.io/launchlane/case-study.html)
- [Review the Amplitude analytics plan](analytics-plan.md)

This concept demonstrates problem framing, JTBD, focused MVP scope, outcome and guardrail measures, and validation planning. It has no customer research or outcome validation yet. All accounts, statuses, and charts are fictional; there is no backend, authentication, enforced access, or real invitation/email.

Optional Amplitude events are sent only after visitor opt-in. Names and notes are excluded; autocapture, session replay, and IP-address collection are disabled. These events describe portfolio demo usage, not customer adoption or product outcomes. Do not enter real customer, employee, confidential, or personal information.
